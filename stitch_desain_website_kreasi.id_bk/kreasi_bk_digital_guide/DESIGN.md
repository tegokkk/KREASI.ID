## Brand & Style
The design system supports a modern guidance and counseling (*Bimbingan dan Konseling*) platform tailored for Indonesian junior high school students (SMP, ages 12–15). It channels the tactile warmth and accessibility of contemporary student workbooks and creative digital editorial publications. 

The personality is encouraging, approachable, safe, and emotionally clear—never clinical, bureaucratic, or intimidating. It addresses the student conversationally with respectful warmth ("kamu"), transforming introspective exercises, potential mapping, and self-checks into inviting interactive moments.

The design philosophy combines clean structural minimalism with tactile warmth and expressive typography. Bold, friendly headlines pair with generous breathing room, high-contrast readable type, pastel step-coding, and soft pill surfaces to create a structured yet reassuring digital environment.

## Colors
The palette relies on deliberate surface color blocking to organize learning steps and ease cognitive load:
- **Primary (`#304BE4`, Cobalt):** Anchor color used for global hero containers, key interactive CTAs, active selections, and focus indicators.
- **Secondary (`#FF7EA1`, Pink):** Warm expressive accent used in decorative illustrations, stickers, tags, and celebratory details.
- **Tertiary (`#A2EFE3`, Mint):** High-contrast energetic accent for critical hero buttons, forward action items, and milestone highlights.
- **Canvas (`#FFFAEE`, Cream):** The foundation background for page scaffolding, offering a warm paper-like tone that softens contrast compared to stark white.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`, White):** Used for cards, interactive answer sheets, and structured content modules floating on the cream canvas.
- **Ink (`#241831`) & Muted (`#594D63`):** High-contrast, deep purple-tinted neutral for optimal legibility across all pastel and white surfaces.

### Activity Step-Color Mapping
Each guidance module is mapped to an identifiable pastel tint:
- **Langkah 1 (Self-check):** `#E8EDFF` (Biru Pucat)
- **Langkah 2 (Kenali Potensi):** `#FFF1C5` (Kuning Pucat)
- **Langkah 3 (Mengembangkan Potensi):** `#E5F4D8` (Hijau Pucat)
- **Langkah 4 (Kuis Eksplorasi):** `#F0E8FF` (Lilac Pucat)
- **Langkah 5 (Refleksi Diri):** `#FFE6EE` (Pink Pucat)
- **Langkah 6 (Action Plan):** `#A2EFE3` (Mint Cerah)

## Typography
Typography creates a playful yet structured dynamic between the expressive **Bricolage Grotesque** and the grounded clarity of **DM Sans**:
- **Display & Headings:** Rendered in Bricolage Grotesque with optical kerning and tight negative tracking (`-0.035em` to `-0.015em`) on larger sizes to impart energy, warmth, and personality.
- **Body & Forms:** Rendered in DM Sans with open letter shapes, comfortable x-height, and ample line-height (`1.55`–`1.6`) to ensure effortless reading for adolescents.
- **Reading Length:** Long-form content, reflection prompts, and form sheets must strictly enforce an optimal reading measure between 60 and 70 characters per line (max container width: `720px`).
- **Metadata & Step Counters:** Step indicators such as *"Langkah 2 dari 6"* use `label-sm` or `body-sm` uppercase with slight letter-spacing to establish clear orientation without visual distraction.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined fluid container system capped at a maximum width of `1200px`:
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column or 3-column modular activity grid with `24px` gutters and minimum `32px` page margins. Hero layouts utilize an asymmetric split (60% reading / 40% graphic element).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 2-column card flow with `16px` gutters and `24px` page margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layout with `12px` to `16px` component gaps and `16px` outer page margins.
- **Vertical Cadence:** Section spacing is standard at `4rem` (64px) on desktop and scales gracefully down to `2.5rem` (40px) on mobile viewports.
- **Touch Areas:** All clickable surfaces adhere strictly to a minimum target height of `44px` (`48px` standard for inputs and buttons).

## Elevation & Depth
The design intentionally avoids heavy, synthetic skeuomorphic drop shadows in favor of a clean, flat editorial workbook feel:
- **Tonal Color Layering:** Depth is created primarily through flat color plane transitions: warm cream canvas (`#FFFAEE`) sits below crisp white containers (`#FFFFFF`), which frame pastel interactive panels and deep cobalt hero sections.
- **Selective Ambient Shadows:** Real-world floating depth is reserved exclusively for modal views, popovers, and mobile flyout drawers using a soft, ink-tinted diffusion: `0 8px 24px rgba(36, 24, 49, 0.08)`.
- **Tactile Outlines:** Cards and input controls rely on crisp, high-contrast borders (`1px solid #E4DAE1` for structural separators, `1px solid #81778E` for form fields) rather than box-shadow blurs.
- **Decorative Editorial Halftones:** Subtle geometric dot matrix patterns (*tekstur titik cetak*) appear sparingly in hero container corners and illustration backgrounds to evoke modern printed stationery.

## Shapes
The shape language combines friendly, rounded geometry with tailored radii across UI tiers:
- **Pill / Capsule (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Used universally across all standard action buttons (primary, secondary, and hero CTA buttons), step chips, and status badges.
- **Cards & Surfaces (`20px`):** Used for all activity cards, quiz containers, and reading panels to ensure a gentle, child-friendly contour without appearing childish.
- **Hero Containers:** Large focal containers use `32px` corner rounding on desktop viewports, softening to `24px` on mobile viewports.
- **Form Controls (`12px`):** Text inputs, textareas, and helper note boxes use a refined `12px` radius that maintains internal structure alongside pill-shaped buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Capsule shape (`border-radius: 9999px`), `48px` height, horizontal padding `24px`. Cobalt background (`#304BE4`), cream label (`#FFFAEE`), font `label-md`. Hover shifts to `#2337B7` over `120ms ease-out`.
- **Hero CTA Button:** Mint background (`#A2EFE3`), deep ink label (`#241831`). Designed to maximize punch against the cobalt hero background.
- **Secondary Button:** Capsule shape, cream background (`#FFFAEE`) or transparent with a solid cobalt border (`2px solid #304BE4`), cobalt text.
- **Focus States:** Every button receives an explicit focus ring: `2px solid #304BE4` with `3px` offset on light backgrounds; `2px solid #FFFAEE` with `3px` offset when placed on cobalt surfaces.

### Activity & Content Cards
- **Base Style:** Rounded `20px`, minimum padding `24px` (`space-lg`), zero shadow in resting state.
- **Thematic Cards:** The background corresponds to the step identity (e.g., `#E8EDFF` for Self-Check, `#FFF1C5` for Kenali Potensi). Titles are rendered in `title-md` (Bricolage Grotesque), followed by body copy in `body-md` (Ink `#241831`).
- **Interactive Option Cards:** Used within quizzes and self-assessment forms. In resting state, white surface with `1px solid #E4DAE1`. When selected, transitions to `activity-blue` (`#E8EDFF`) with a `2px solid #304BE4` border and an active radio or checkmark badge.

### Chips & Step Indicators
- **Step Pill:** Small capsule container (`rounded-full`), `32px` height, padding `12px` horizontal. Used for step breadcrumbs (e.g., "Langkah 1", "Selesai").
- **Tags & Badges:** Subtle pastel fills with contrasting ink text in `label-sm`.

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Inputs & Textareas:** `rounded: 12px`, `48px` single-line height, `12px` internal padding, `104px` min-height for textareas. Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), resting border `1px solid #81778E`, input text in `body-md` (`#241831`).
- **Focus State:** Transitions to `2px solid #304BE4` with zero horizontal jump.
- **Error State:** Border becomes `2px solid #A22A46` with accompanying surface tint `#FFF0F4` and error helper text in `#A22A46`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** `20px × 20px`, `rounded: 6px`, resting border `2px solid #81778E`. Selected state displays solid `#304BE4` with a crisp white checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** `20px × 20px` circle. Selected state displays a cobalt concentric dot (`10px` inner diameter) inside a white ring with cobalt outline.
- **Touch Target:** Bound within a minimum `44px × 44px` interactive area.

### Personal Reflection Notes ("Catatanku")
- Dedicated notebook-style panel (`#A2EFE3` or `#FFF1C5` surface) with `rounded: 12px` and `16px` padding, featuring an encouraging prompt icon and a conversational guide note for the student.
